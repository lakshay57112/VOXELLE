Bolkar schedule banayein. Dawai ke time par yeh unse Hindi mein baat karta hai, jawab sunta hai, aur chhoot jaye to aapko batata hai.

Beta / Beti ke liye

Main parivaar se hoon

Schedule bolkar banayein, dose ka hisaab dekhein, alerts paayein.
Papa / Mummy ke liye

Main dawai leta / leti hoon

Bade button, saaf awaaz. Bas "haan" ya "baad mein" boliye.

Kaise kaam karta hai

  1. Parivaar bolta hai: "Papa ko subah BP ki goli khaane ke baad, raat ko sugar ki goli."
  2. App schedule padhkar sunata hai. Aap "sahi hai" dabate hain.
  3. Time par phone par notification aata hai, awaaz mein yaad dilata hai, aur jawab sunta hai.
  4. Dawai chhoot jaye to parivaar ko turant message jaata hai.

Yeh app sirf yaad dilata hai aur likhta hai. Yeh dawai, dose ya sehat ki salah nahi deta. Koi takleef ho to parivaar aur doctor se baat karein.
